LAAU Portal

The Student & Executive Portal brings your assigned credentials, licensed learning resources, recognitions and professional services together in one place.

Sign in to LAAU Portal Contact portal support

What you can access

Credential portfolio

View credentials associated with your account, including available university certificates, trainer certificates and digital badges.

Licensed learning resources

Access official resources assigned to you through your eligible programmes and services.

Professional services

Find the assessments, recognitions and executive services available through your portal account.

Availability depends on your account permissions, enrolments and assigned services. Signing in does not automatically grant a credential or access to every resource.

How sign-in works

Use your registered Google account or request a passwordless sign-in link at your registered email address. LAAU Portal uses Firebase Authentication to manage sign-in.

If your expected services are missing, contact support@laau.university.

Privacy and terms

Read the privacy policy and terms before using the portal. For questions about your information, contact support@laau.university.
