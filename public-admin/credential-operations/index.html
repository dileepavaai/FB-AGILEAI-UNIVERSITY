Governance Notice

Credential Operations is the authoritative administrative gateway for credential lifecycle and operational administration activities within Agile AI University.

Operational surfaces are intentionally accessed through this gateway to support governance consistency, auditability, navigation simplicity, and future platform scalability.

Credential Asset Audit provides a read-only operational view of credential asset readiness. It does not generate, publish, replace, or modify credential artifacts.

Existing operational URLs remain canonical and unchanged. This gateway introduces no changes to credential issuance, registry records, batch processing, or platform security controls.
